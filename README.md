## What "brandless" means

No front cover. No back cover. No logo, no header, no footer, no watermark, no URL down the
margin. Each page is the printable and nothing else.

This is the part that makes the CC0 licence mean something. A public domain file with our
branding across it is only nominally free — you could not put it in a book you sell, hand it to
a client, or bundle it into your own pack without stripping our name off it first. There is
nothing here to strip.

So: print them, sell them, put them in your own product, hand them out at a workshop. No
attribution, no permission, no royalty.

## Printing

Everything is **A4** unless the filename says otherwise. Most files print acceptably on US
Letter with *fit to page*, though margins shift slightly.

Puzzle pages and their solutions are separate files, so you can print a book of puzzles without
the answers falling out in the middle of it.

## Found a problem?

Worth reporting: a page that prints cut off, a maze with no solution or more than one, a
solution that does not match its puzzle, a file that opens blank.

Not a bug: a puzzle being harder than you expected.

Include the filename. See [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md).
