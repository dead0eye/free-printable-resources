# Code of conduct

## The short version

Be decent to people. This is a free project run by a small team; nobody here is being paid to
absorb rudeness.

## What that means

**Welcome:** questions at any level of experience, disagreement about technical decisions,
blunt reports that something is broken, and people whose first language is not English.

**Not welcome:** personal attacks, harassment of any kind, demeaning comments about someone's
identity or background, deliberately derailing a thread, and treating a maintainer's unpaid
time as an entitlement.

Being right about the technical point does not make the rest of it acceptable.

## Scope

This applies to issues, pull requests, discussions, and anywhere else the project is
represented.

## If something goes wrong

Report it via [https://x.com/getfreeebooks](https://x.com/getfreeebooks). Reports are read by the maintainers and handled
privately.

Maintainers may edit, hide or delete comments, close threads, and block accounts. For anything
serious we will not warn twice.
